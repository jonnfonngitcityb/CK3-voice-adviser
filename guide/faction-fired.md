# Decision

A faction just fired, or it is about to. What are the real responses, and what does each one cost?

# Applies when

You are the liege, and the faction is against you. Feudal realm. The three that matter in an 867 kingdom are Liberty, Independence, and Claimant.

This page is not for a war you declared on a neighbor. That is `this-war.md`.

# Do this

1. Open the faction from the alert. Read its type, its discontent, and its members.
2. If discontent is not yet 100, pull members off before it delivers. A vassal should not be inside it at 80 or more opinion, or if he is your friend, lover, or ally, or if you have a strong hook or a truce with him. A terrified vassal stays out unless discontent is already rising.
3. If the ultimatum is here, choose accept or refuse. An unjust imprisonment can make them deliver early. Accepting costs 20 Dread and Legitimacy on top of that faction's own demand.

# What each choice changes

- Accept a Liberty ultimatum. Crown authority drops one step. You lose 200 Prestige. You also lose 20 Dread and Legitimacy. A drop from Limited to Autonomous takes away title revocation and vassal retraction.
- Refuse Liberty. It becomes a civil war. Members stop paying you taxes and levies, and you lose some powers, including imprisonment, while it lasts. The war declaration lists what a win gives back.
- Accept Independence. Those members leave the realm. You lose 150 Prestige, plus the 20 Dread and Legitimacy.
- Refuse Independence. Same kind of civil war. You are fighting to keep them.
- Accept a Claimant ultimatum. The claimant takes the title they claimed. Every member gains a weak hook on that claimant. If the title is your primary title, you are no longer its holder.
- Refuse a Claimant ultimatum. Civil war for that title. Winning keeps it. Read the war goal for what a win does beyond keeping the title. Do not add imprisonments or revocations the goal does not list.

Before the ultimatum, swaying one angry powerful vassal to 80 opinion, or allying him, removes that member. One ally is enough to change the military power. It does not delete a faction that still has other members.

# Suggested play

Do not accept a Claimant ultimatum for the primary title. That is the loss. A Liberty drop of one crown-authority step is often cheaper than a civil war you cannot win this year. Independence is the middle case: accept it only for a vassal whose land you cannot hold and whose faction would pull half the realm in with him.

# Why

Discontent reaching 100 is what delivers the ultimatum. Military power is their troops against yours. The panel shows both. Player-led factions can deliver early. An AI faction delivers at 100, or early if you imprison someone without a just reason.

A vassal who is underage also should not join. If the panel shows someone the join rules forbid, the tooltip on that member is the reason, and it wins over this page.

# If this does not match

Read the faction type on the panel. Populist and peasant factions are counties, not vassals, and their demands are not the three above. If the type is one of those, this page does not cover the demand. Read the ultimatum text.

If you are the member, not the liege, this page is the liege's choice. Your side delivers the ultimatum from the faction window.
