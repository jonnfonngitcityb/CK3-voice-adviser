# Decision

This feudal war is already on. What should the army be, and what changes if I white-peace, enforce, or call the ally?

# Applies when

A war you are in, shown on the war banner. Feudal or your feudal liege's war. A raid with no war banner is `vikings-at-the-border.md`, not this page.

# Do this

1. Open the war banner and read the goal. The prize is the tooltip on Enforce Demands, not a guess from the target's map color.
2. Open Military. Raise the men-at-arms. Raise levies if the men-at-arms alone are smaller than the enemy stack on the war screen.
3. Call an ally from the Call to War interaction on that character. The acceptance tooltip is the reason they join or refuse. Their army is theirs. It helps you only while it is in the war and moving with the fight.

Marshal tasks that change this war while it lasts:

- Train Commanders. Plus 1% men-at-arms damage and 1% toughness per point of his Martial, growing monthly up to that cap.
- Organize Army. Minus 1% raised-army maintenance per Martial, plus 2% levy reinforcement and 2% garrison per Martial.

# What each choice changes

- Enforce Demands when the button is available. You get the prize on that button. A claim war gives the claimed title to the claimant named on the goal. A de jure war gives the de jure land on the goal. The button lists the prestige before you click.
- White Peace. You do not take the land. If the goal was an unpressed claim, that claim becomes pressed. Ambitious and Arrogant characters gain Stress for signing white peace in an offensive war. The button lists the prestige and the stress before you click.
- Surrender. The other side's enforce result happens to you. Use it only when the banner shows a loss you cannot reverse before the score hits their enforce number.
- Call the ally. If they accept, another army enters on your side and you do not command it as if it were your men-at-arms. If they refuse, the tooltip is the cost or the relation you are missing. A refused call does not change the goal.
- Hire mercenaries from Military if the raised army is still smaller than the enemy. They cost gold up front and while raised. The contract on the mercenary company is the duration. This does not change the goal. It changes whether you can reach the score.

# Suggested play

Fight the war you declared, with men-at-arms raised first. Call the ally before you are losing, not after the score is already their enforce. White peace a claim war only when keeping the war would cost the realm and a pressed claim is still useful next generation. Do not white peace a war whose only prize was land you need this year.

# Why

Men-at-arms are the regiments you recruit. Levies are the larger, weaker mass raised with them. The regiment tooltip shows damage, toughness, and any station bonus from the holding you assigned. Station the regiment, then read that number. There is no single feudal composition that the rules name as correct.

Knights add to the same army. Train Commanders can improve one. It can also wound or kill a commander. That risk is on the task.

# If this does not match

If there is no war banner, it is not this page. A torch and a raiding army are the raid page. If Enforce Demands lists a different title than the one you wanted, that listed title is the prize. The goal was chosen at declaration.
