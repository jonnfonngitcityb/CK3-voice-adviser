# Decision

Raiders, or a tribal neighbor, are on a feudal realm. What can I actually do, and what does fighting or hiring change?

# Applies when

A raiding army is on your land and there is no war banner, or a neighbor is hostile because they can raid. You are feudal. A Catholic feudal king is the case this page is written for.

If a war banner is open, this is a war. Use `this-war.md` and `need-a-casus-belli.md`. A raid is not a war.

# Do this

1. Select the enemy army. If it is raiding, it cannot move while it loots the holding. That is the window.
2. Open Military and raise men-at-arms onto that army before it leaves for home. If you beat it before it crosses back inside its own borders, the loot it is carrying is recovered.
3. If your raised army is too small, hire a mercenary company from Military and send it to the same battle. There is no button on this page that pays the raider to leave.

You cannot raid them back unless you yourself have a raid right. A feudal Catholic does not get one from feudal government. Raiding needs something like tribal government, an unreformed faith, the Practiced Pirates tradition, or another right on the raise-army tooltip. If Raise as Raiders is missing, you do not have it.

# What each choice changes

- Fight them on the holding. They are stuck while raiding. A win before they reach their own land returns the loot. Your holdings still took the raid's damage if they finished the loot. The army panel shows whether loot is still on them.
- Fight them late. If they are already moving home with the loot, a win inside your land can still recover it, until they enter their borders. After that, the loot is gone. This page does not give you a second way to get it back.
- Hire mercenaries. You spend the gold on the company entry. You gain a stack for that contract. You do not gain a truce, a claim, or their land.
- Ignore a small raid. They take the holding's loot and leave. A Marches building in the duchy cuts the extra raid-intent effects in half. The upgraded Marches building removes those extras. It does not stop the raid.
- Declare a real war. Only if Declare War shows a goal. Winning that war gives the tooltip's prize, not "the raiders stop forever." Use the war pages. Do not declare a war you do not have a goal for.

# Suggested play

For an 867 feudal king on the coast, raise for the raid that is already stuck on a holding, and hire mercenaries only when that army is larger than yours. Do not start a kingdom war just to answer a loot stack. After the battle, look at which duchy they entered. A Marches building there is the lasting answer, not a new law.

# Why

Raiding needs no war. The raider raises an army as raiders. Faiths with Peace of God as a core tenet block raiding. The raider's army carries loot based on its size, and the raise has a raid intent that decides whether the loot becomes gold, prestige, piety, or dread. Pillage, the common one, turns loot into gold.

While the raiding army is selected, holdings show how much loot they still hold. Maintenance for a raiding army is lower for them. That does not change your side.

Same-faith feudal neighbors are not this page. They need a claim or a de jure goal like anyone else.

# If this does not match

If the army is moving and the portrait says it is a war, leave this page. If Raise as Raiders is present on your own Military button, you have a raid right this page did not assume, and the tooltip names which one.

The army panel's loot number is the fact. If it is already empty, the recovery fight will not pay you.
