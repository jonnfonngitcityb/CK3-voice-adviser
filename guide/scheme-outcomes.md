# Decision

I am running a scheme, or someone is running one on me. What does success change, and what does getting caught change?

# Applies when

A scheme in the scheme list, or the Realm Priest's Fabricate Claim task. Feudal court. Political schemes are for administrative, celestial, and meritocratic governments. They are not this page.

Fabricate Claim is not a scheme on this patch. It is a council task.

# Do this

1. Open the scheme. Read its type: Hostile or Personal.
2. For a hostile scheme, read Success Chance, Secrecy, Breaches, and Advantage. You may execute once Advantage is at least 5. Spending 10, 15, or 20 Advantage adds 5, 10, or 20 Success Chance, and cannot push the chance above the scheme's max.
3. For Fabricate Claim, open the council and read the Realm Priest's task progress. Do not look for it on the Chancellor.

# What each choice changes

- Murder succeeds. The target dies. His heir takes his titles and his place in any partition. You gain Stress if you are Honest (+40), Just (+40), or Compassionate (+80).
- Murder is discovered. The chance to be discovered on execution is the missing Secrecy. Each month, the chance of a Breach is a tenth of the missing Secrecy. At 5 Breaches the scheme fails. A discovered hostile scheme can cost Legitimacy. The exposure event states the opinion hit.
- Murder while you are at war with the target. Success Chance takes −50. Abduction takes −500, and Abduction also needs the Kidnapper perk.
- Sway completes. Personal scheme. Success is a 95% chance of +25 opinion with the target, or a 5% chance of +30. The listed failures are a 2% chance to lose 10 opinion, and a 2% chance to lose 10 opinion while the target gains 1 Intrigue and 8 hostile-scheme resistance for 5 years.
- Stop a scheme on you. Set one countermeasure focus. Only one can be active. They cut secrecy or success for a group of schemes and cost something else. The focus tooltip is the trade. The encyclopedia text on that screen is the rule.
- Fabricate Claim reaches 100%. You gain an unpressed claim on that county. A positive event can be a duchy claim instead. A negative event can cost Piety, anger the target, and cost vassal opinion. Speed is 3% a month base, plus 0.2% a month per point of the priest's Learning.
- Claim Throne succeeds. You need the Meritocracy perk, and you must be landed. You gain a pressed claim on your liege's primary title. It does not give you the title by itself.

# Suggested play

Sway a powerful vassal who is in a faction before you murder anyone. Murder a younger heir only when `too-many-heirs.md` says his death actually removes the title from the preview. His son replaces him if he has one. Use the priest for one county, and stop the task if a negative event has already angered a vassal you need.

# Why

Hostile and political schemes track Potential, Secrecy, Breaches, and Advantage. Personal schemes track Success Chance and how long each phase takes. Potential caps Success Chance. Skills add up to 30 Potential, agents add the rest, and Potential does not go above 95% except on contract schemes.

An agent's value depends on how close they are to the target. A spouse is +30 Success Chance. The target's spymaster, if an agent, adds as much success chance as that spymaster's Intrigue. Someone terrified of the target will not join unless you use a strong hook.

Grace is the opening period, normally six months, where secrecy does not apply. Court of Shadows raises grace to ten months.

# If this does not match

The scheme window's type label wins. If Fabricate Claim is not on the Realm Priest, check the Court Chaplain. If neither has it, this task is not available and the council list is the stop. Do not send the Chancellor to fabricate a claim on patch 1.20.
